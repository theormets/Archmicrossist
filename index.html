How to read a match

Upload a JPG/JPEG or an opaque, 8-bit, non-interlaced PNG. Search considers all references in the current library, including Hold records and all recorded microscopy methods. The reported microscopy method is shown on each result; the uploaded image’s method is not automatically identified. Names and labels do not determine visual ranking. Hold records remain unverified. One result per recorded sample. Images are display previews; consult source papers for originals. The microscope banner is decorative generated artwork, not analytical data.
